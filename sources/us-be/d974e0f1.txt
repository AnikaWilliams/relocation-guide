These pages are primarily intended for non-EU foreigners who wish to stay in Belgium for more than 90 days in order to: study at a recognized higher education institution and, at the end of their studies, obtain a degree recognized in Belgium; stay for an additional year after completing their studies in order to find a job or start a business with the aim of obtaining a residence permit for work purposes. These pages are also intended for non-EU foreigners who wish to stay in Belgium for more than 90 days as part of a student exchange program or mobility program. (Under construction) Finally, these pages are intended for non-EU foreigners who wish to stay in Belgium for more than 90 days to pursue training at a higher education institution that is not recognized and not authorized to issue diplomas recognized in Belgium.
